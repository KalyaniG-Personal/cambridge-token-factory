Model and assumptions

Compute (per prompt)

  • 2035 GPU: 2.5 kW, 40 PFLOPS FP8 / 80 FP4 dense, 40 TB/s and 576 GB HBM, idle at 30% of TDP.
  • Prefill is compute-bound at 62% utilization; cached prefix tokens are free.
  • Decode step time = max(weights + KV reads ÷ bandwidth, FLOPs ÷ compute) + 0.25 ms. Speculative decoding verifies 4 drafts, accepts 2.6, costs 12% extra.
  • CPU, network and storage add 12%; GPUs serve 72% of the time.
  • Leakage: chip power × (1 + 0.55% per K of junction above 65 °C), floored at −6%. Junction cap 95 °C.

Facility (8,760 hours)

  • Air temperatures: Boston monthly normals + 1 K, ±5 K daily swing. Bedrock at 50 m: 12 °C, warming up to 4 K under load.
  • Free cooling when sink + approach ≤ supply; partial up to return (supply + 10 K); chillers at 55% of Carnot for the rest.
  • Conversion efficiency: AC chain 89.3%, 800 V DC 96.6%.
  • On-site water: cooling towers 1.8 L/kWh of IT; dry coolers 0.08 L/kWh (spray assist on hot days); closed ground loops 0.
  • District network takes up to 60 MWth, scaled by monthly heat demand. Heat pumps lift return water to 75 °C at 50% of Carnot.

Metrics

  • Net energy credits delivered heat at the electricity a city air-source heat pump (COP 3) would need, and charges our own heat-pump power.
  • Grid: $82/MWh, 68% carbon-free hour by hour (120 gCO₂/kWh average); the remainder is gas-heavy at 380 g/kWh. Heat sold at $35/MWhth.
  • Clean-power slider: hourly matching via offshore wind, storage and clean firm power. Price premium rises steeply toward 100%, reaching +45% (Princeton ZERO Lab range).
  • Grid water: 3.5 L consumed per kWh of thermal generation; wind, solar and storage use ~0.
  • All tokens are counted as generated (output) tokens; input tokens are part of the prompt's cost.
