Model and assumptions

Thermal solver (ground truth)

Steady 2D conduction in the die + vapor-chamber lid on a 48 × 48 grid (0.83 mm cells), with vertical conduction through TIM and cold plate to 35 °C coolant. Edges are adiabatic. Solved by red-black SOR (ω = 1.92).

Σnbr Glat(Tn − T) − Gv(T − Tc) + P = 0
Glat = k·t = 12 W/K · Gv = h·Δx² , h = 5×10⁴ W/m²K

GPU dies carry a hot shader core: the central 36% of area runs at 1.6× the die's edge power density.

Surrogate and search

  • Input: power map average-pooled to 12 × 12 blocks, plus total and peak cell power (146 features). Permutation-free: it sees heat, not chiplet IDs.
  • Network: 146 → 48 → 24 → 1, leaky ReLU, Adam, trained in your browser.
  • Objective: max over the three workloads of predicted Tmax (robust design).
  • Moves: shift, rotate, swap; any move breaking a rule is rejected.
  • Active learning: each round runs three anneals; their winners are solved exactly for all workloads and added to the training set. This catches layouts that fool the surrogate (predicted cool, actually hot) and teaches it not to repeat the mistake.
